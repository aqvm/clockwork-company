# Guard

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Guard is the straightforward baseline defensive job.

- **Skill:** `Shield Wall`, self-guard with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Guard Training`, +1 guard armor.
- **Reaction:** `Hold Fast`, gain 3 armor below its HP threshold, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +2 max HP and +1 armor per level.

Implementation: `clockwork-company/resources/jobs/guard.tres`
