# Cutpurse

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Cutpurse is a fast physical attacker.

- **Skill:** `Pocket Sand`, attack with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Dirty Edge`, +1 attack damage with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Vanish Step`, gain 2 armor when damaged, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 physical damage and +1 action speed per level.
