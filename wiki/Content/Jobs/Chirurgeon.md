# Chirurgeon

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Chirurgeon is an armored healer.

- **Skill:** `Field Stitch`, heal the lowest-HP ally with +2 amount.
- **Passive:** `Clean Hands`, +2 healing with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Reflex Splint`, gain 3 armor below its HP threshold, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 max HP, +1 magic damage, and +1 armor per level.
