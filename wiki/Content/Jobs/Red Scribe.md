# Red Scribe

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Red Scribe is a fast blood-themed healer.

- **Skill:** `Red Stitch`, heal the lowest-HP ally with +2 amount.
- **Passive:** `Blood Margin`, +1 healing with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Ink Clot`, heal self for 3 below half HP, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 magic damage and +1 action speed per level.
