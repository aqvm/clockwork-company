# Lamplighter

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Lamplighter is a fast support healer with damage retaliation.

- **Skill:** `Lamp Touch`, heal the lowest-HP ally with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Clear Flame`, +1 healing with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Backdraft`, damage the attacker for 2 when damaged, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 max HP, +1 magic damage, and +1 action speed per level.
