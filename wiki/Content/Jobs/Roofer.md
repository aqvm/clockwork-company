# Roofer

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Roofer is a fast physical duelist.

- **Skill:** `Drop Strike`, attack with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `High Ground`, +1 attack damage with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Roll Off`, gain 2 armor when damaged, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 physical damage and +1 action speed per level.
