# Ratcatcher

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Ratcatcher is a physical hunter with a stronger but slower-growth attack profile.

- **Skill:** `Snare Jab`, attack the frontmost enemy with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Cornered Prey`, +2 attack damage with a 2-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Trap Spring`, damage the attacker for 2 when damaged, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +2 physical damage per level.
