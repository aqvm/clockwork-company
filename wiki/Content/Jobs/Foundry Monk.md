# Foundry Monk

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Foundry Monk is a durable self-guarding job, distinct from the designed [[Monk]].

- **Skill:** `Bank the Furnace`, self-guard with +2 amount.
- **Passive:** `Tempered Breath`, +1 guard armor with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Turtle Furnace`, gain 8 armor below 35% HP, 6-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +2 max HP and +1 armor per level.
