# Archivist

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Archivist is a fast support healer built around [[Foretell]].

- **Skill:** `Margin Note`, heal the lowest-HP ally with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Read Ahead`, grants Forecast capability.
- **Reaction:** `Footnote Ward`, heal self for 4 below half HP, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 magic damage and +1 action speed per level.
