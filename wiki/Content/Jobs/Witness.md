# Witness

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Witness is a flexible magic-oriented attacker with self-recovery.

- **Skill:** `Remembered Blow`, attack the frontmost enemy with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Careful Eye`, +1 attack damage with a 2-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Testimony`, heal self for 2 below half HP, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 max HP and +1 magic damage per level.
