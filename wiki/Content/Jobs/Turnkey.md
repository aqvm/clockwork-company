# Turnkey

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Turnkey is an armor-heavy guard/control job.

- **Skill:** `Lockstep`, self-guard with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Key-Ring Guard`, +1 guard armor with a 1-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Door Slam`, damage the attacker for 2 when damaged, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 max HP and +2 armor per level.
