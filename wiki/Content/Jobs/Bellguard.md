# Bellguard

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Bellguard is a slow, durable guard specialist.

- **Skill:** `Bell Brace`, self-guard with +2 amount.
- **Passive:** `Rung-Stance`, +2 guard armor with a 2-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Tolling Shell`, gain 5 armor below 45% HP, 5-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +3 max HP, +1 armor, and -1 action speed per level.
