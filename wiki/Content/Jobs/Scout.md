# Scout

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Scout is a fast baseline physical attacker.

- **Skill:** `Opening Cut`, attack with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `Sharpened Edge`, +1 attack damage.
- **Reaction:** `Slip Away`, gain 2 armor when damaged, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 physical damage and +1 action speed per level.
