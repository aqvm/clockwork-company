# Apprentice

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Apprentice is a fast magic trainee with a small attack skill and general healing support.

- **Skill:** `Lesson Spark`, attack with +1 amount.
- **Passive:** `First Aid`, +2 healing.
- **Reaction:** `Panic Lesson`, heal self for 3 below its HP threshold, 3-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +1 magic damage and +1 action speed per level.
