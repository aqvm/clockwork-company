# Debt Knight

> [!warning] Runnable prototype, not a final job design.

Debt Knight is an armored physical attacker with retaliation.

- **Skill:** `Call the Debt`, attack with +2 amount.
- **Passive:** `Collateral`, +2 guard armor with a 2-turn cooldown.
- **Reaction:** `Payment Due`, damage the attacker for 3 below its HP threshold, 4-turn cooldown.
- **Growth:** +2 max HP, +1 physical damage, and +1 armor per level.
