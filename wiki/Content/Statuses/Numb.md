# Numb

**Polarity:** [[Ailments|Ailment]]
**State:** Implemented
**Stacking:** `Refresh`, one stack

Numb prevents the afflicted unit from triggering reactions while active. The combat log explains when an otherwise-valid reaction is suppressed.

## Tactical Role

Numb is a short control effect that opens a window to act without retaliation or defensive reaction interference.

## Designed Content Links

- The designed Cryomancer applies Numb together with [[Frost]].

## Related

- [[Confusion]]: disrupts tactic selection rather than reactions.
- [[Ward]]: can prevent Numb from applying.
- [[Renewal]]: rewards removing Numb.
