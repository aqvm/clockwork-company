# Boons

Boons are positive [[Statuses]].

- [[Reconstitution]] converts recent damage into delayed recovery.
- [[Regeneration]] provides steady turn-start healing.
- [[Renewal]] rewards removing [[Ailments]].
- [[Ward]] prevents incoming [[Ailments]].

Boons can also be counted by content using `Target Unique Boons`; repeated stacks still count as one unique boon type.

See [[Statuses]] for shared duration and stacking rules.
