# Fever Choir Rehearsal

Fever Choir Rehearsal is a tier-2 standalone test scenario built to exercise a synergistic enemy party in the Combat Lab or scenario runner.

- **Party size:** 4
- **Recommended levels:** 2-3
- **Encounters:** Fever Choir Rehearsal
- **Rule:** none
- **Rewards:** none
- **Enemy plan:** Burning pressure from Ash-Censer Ilyra, Frost/Numb conversion from Rime Bailiff Oren, corrupt healing from Mire Confessor Sable, and tempo support from Bell Cantor Voss.
