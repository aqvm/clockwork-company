# Monk

Monk is an unarmed support attacker that absorbs allied burdens, recovers through untouched discipline, and seals repeatedly attacked opponents.

## Intended Kit

- Forbid weapons and gain unarmed attack damage.
- Attack, then apply [[Regeneration]] to the lowest-HP ally.
- Transfer allied ailments to self.
- Heal after three completed actions without being targeted by an enemy attack.
- Seal an enemy's next attack after three consecutive attacks against it.

This replaces the earlier Foundry Monk prototype rather than preserving it as a separate current job.
