# The Spike

The Spike is an armorless retaliation tank that protects allies by becoming the most dangerous target to attack.

## Intended Kit

- Disable its own armor and retaliate when physically damaged.
- Prevent and reflect a lethal physical attack on a cooldown.
- Attack, then Fortify incoming damage for several actions.
- Temporarily redirect enemy attacks to self.

Fortified prevention resolves before the lethal reaction. Deferred damage cannot be deferred again.
