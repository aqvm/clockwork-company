# Cryomancer

Cryomancer sets up physical burst with [[Frost]], suppresses reactions with [[Numb]], and slows the enemy team as Frost accumulates.

## Intended Kit

- Apply Frost and Numb to an enemy.
- Dynamically slow all enemies based on total enemy Frost.
- Retaliate against physical attackers with Frost.
- `Cold Blood` converts an enemy's Frost stacks into [[Bleed]] and consumes the Frost.

No new resolver capability is currently required.
