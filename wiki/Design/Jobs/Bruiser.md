# Bruiser

Bruiser develops high HP, intercepts attacks aimed at allies, and turns repeated physical punishment into accelerating tempo.

## Intended Kit

- Strong maximum-HP growth.
- Strike twice, then delay the target.
- Redirect an attack aimed at another ally.
- Hasten after taking physical damage, bounded by the global double-speed cap.
- Deal physical damage proportional to a target's remaining HP.

The double strike is two complete attack resolutions. `Stun` is a named [[Timeline and Action Scheduling|timeline delay]], not a status.
