# Sanguinist

Sanguinist deliberately accepts ailments and converts actual HP loss into tempo, recovery, and magic protection.

## Intended Kit

- Apply [[Bleed]] to an enemy and self.
- Gain battle-long haste after positive ailment-caused HP loss.
- Heal when an enemy dies with Bleed, scaling from its max HP and Bleed stacks.
- Grant Energy Shield to the lowest-HP bleeding ally based on recent damage.

Absorbed, prevented, or deferred damage does not qualify as `Ailment Damaged`. [[Rot]] qualifies only when reducing maximum HP also lowers current HP.
