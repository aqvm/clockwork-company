# Enthalpyst

Enthalpyst reflects hostile temperature effects and converts dangerous [[Frost]] buildup into a physical response.

## Intended Kit

- Apply [[Burning]] and Frost together.
- Gather enemy Burning onto one target with `Chimney Effect`.
- Mirror externally applied Burning or Frost stacks back to their source.
- Strike an enemy when it reaches 10 Frost, triggering Frost amplification and shattering it.

Frost currently amplifies post-armor physical damage; whether that ordering makes armor too strong a counter remains a balance watchpoint.
