# Paladin

Paladin combines hybrid damage, selective ailment conversion, and sustained party recovery.

## Intended Kit

- Split one attack evenly between physical and magic damage.
- Maintain [[Reconstitution]] on allied units while alive.
- Replace one selected incoming ailment with a deterministic-random boon.
- `Spiritual Barrier` grants once-per-battle armor based on an ally's unique boons.

Maintained Reconstitution returns after consumption/removal while the Paladin lives, without invalidating independently gained copies.
