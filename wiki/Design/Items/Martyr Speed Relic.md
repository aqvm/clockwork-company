# Martyr Speed Relic

When an ally is defeated in combat, the wearer's action speed cap is removed for a limited window and the wearer gains a burst of speed.

## Intended Behavior

- Trigger on combat defeat, not permanent campaign death.
- Remove the wearer's action speed cap for 10 actions.
- Grant an immediate authored burst of action speed.
- The window should be defined in deterministic combat terms.

This is item-shaped because it is volatile and scenario-dependent. It creates a dangerous comeback plan without making ally death part of a job's reliable core loop.

## Promising Job Synergies

- [[Executioner]] can turn near-loss states into a finishing rampage.
- [[Sanguinist]] fits the blood/death conversion fantasy without requiring the item for the class to function.
- [[Scout]], [[Roofer]], or [[Cutpurse]] can become cleanup threats when a fight goes badly.

## Open Questions

- Decide whether "10 actions" means global scheduled actions or 10 wearer actions.
- Decide whether multiple ally defeats refresh, stack, or are ignored while active.
