# Battle-Start Energy Shield Relic

At battle start, grant all allies Energy Shield. The wearer gains twice as much.

## Intended Behavior

- Apply an authored amount of Energy Shield to every living ally at battle start.
- Apply twice that amount to the wearer.
- The current version is a candidate because it may be too close to a support job ability unless it gains a sharper constraint or conversion.

This item is strongest if Energy Shield is rare, matchup-specific, or fragile. As written, it risks becoming a broad defensive support tool in an equipment slot.

## Promising Job Synergies

- [[Chronomancer]] can amplify a party plan built around mitigating magic damage.
- [[Paladin]] can use it as a protective barrier artifact.
- [[Bard]] can become interesting if its kit extends temporary boons or buffs.

## Open Questions

- Consider making the shield magic-only, fast-decaying, or converted into speed when broken.
- Decide whether Energy Shield is a status, temporary armor family, or separate prevention layer.
