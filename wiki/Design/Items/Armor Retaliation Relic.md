# Armor Retaliation Relic

When the wearer's armor prevents a physical attack from dealing HP damage, the prevented damage is dealt to an enemy.

## Intended Behavior

- Trigger only when physical attack damage is fully prevented by armor or temporary armor.
- Deal damage equal to the prevented HP damage amount.
- The clearest target is the attacker, unless a later item variant deliberately uses another target rule.

This is item-shaped because tank jobs still provide the armor plan. The item converts successful prevention into offense.

## Promising Job Synergies

- [[Guard]], [[Bellguard]], or [[Turnkey]] can turn guard and armor loops into a damage engine.
- [[Foundry Monk]] can make self-guarding durability feel like a furnace that punishes contact.
- [[Paladin]] can bridge defensive barrier play into offensive pressure.

## Open Questions

- Decide whether partial prevention qualifies or only complete prevention.
- Decide whether the returned damage is physical, magic, direct, or typed by the original attack.
