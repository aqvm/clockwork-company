# Third-Attack Nova Relic

Every third attack action the wearer takes also deals magic damage to all enemies.

## Intended Behavior

- Track completed attack actions by this wearer during the battle.
- On every third qualifying attack, deal an authored amount of magic damage to all living enemies.
- The item should make attack frequency, extra attacks, and tactic ordering matter.

This is item-shaped when its counter rules create buildcraft. The wearer still needs a job that attacks well; the item changes what repeated attacks mean.

## Promising Job Synergies

- [[Scout]], [[Cutpurse]], or [[Roofer]] can use high speed and physical attack loops to produce periodic magic area damage.
- [[Spellsword]] can turn a hybrid attack loop into a more explicit physical-to-magic bridge.
- [[Bruiser]] becomes exciting if double strikes count as multiple attack resolutions.

## Open Questions

- Decide whether counterattacks, reactions, double strikes, or bonus attacks count.
- Decide whether the nova targets all enemies at once as direct damage or creates per-target damage requests.
